## What is TryHackMe?

TryHackMe is a hands-on cybersecurity training platform that provides guided and challenge-based labs covering a wide range of topics — from fundamental Linux and networking to advanced web exploitation, privilege escalation, and defensive security. It offers a safe, legal environment to practice real-world skills and build practical intuition.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Room Writeups** — Step-by-step documentation of how I approached and solved various rooms.
- **Notes & Takeaways** — Key concepts, commands, and techniques learned from each room.
- **Progress Tracking** — Rooms organized by category (e.g., Linux, Web, Network, Privilege Escalation) or by learning path.
- **Screenshots** — Where relevant, visual proof of completion and important steps.

## Why I Use TryHackMe

It complements certifications and theoretical study with hands-on practice. It allows me to build muscle memory for tools and techniques, understand attacker and defender perspectives, and document my growth consistently.
